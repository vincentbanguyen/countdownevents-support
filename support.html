FAQ

How do I add a widget to my Home Screen? Long-press your Home Screen, tap the + button in the top left, search "CountdownEvents", choose a size, and tap Add Widget.
How do I add a Lock Screen widget? Long-press your Lock Screen, tap Customize, select the Lock Screen, tap a widget area, and choose CountdownEvents.
My widget isn't updating. Try removing and re-adding the widget. Ensure the app is not restricted in Settings > General > Background App Refresh.
How do I share an event? Swipe left on an event in the list and tap Share. The recipient can open the .countdown file to import it.
Can I customize the widget appearance? Yes — tap any event to edit it. Use the Background, Text, and Template tabs to customize colors, fonts, photos, and themes.
Is my data private? Yes. All data is stored locally on your device. We collect nothing. See our Privacy Policy.
