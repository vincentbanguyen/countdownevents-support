Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Overview

CountdownEvents is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Storage

All data — including event names, dates, photos, and customization settings — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers.

No Analytics

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. There are no third-party SDKs in the app.

Photos

If you add a photo to an event, it is stored locally on your device. Photos are never uploaded or shared unless you explicitly use the share feature to send an event to someone.

Shared Events

When you share an event via a .countdown file, the recipient receives only the event data you created (title, date, customizations, and photo if included). This is a direct peer-to-peer transfer — no data passes through our servers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device using iOS notification services. No notification data is sent externally.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
